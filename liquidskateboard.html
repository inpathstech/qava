Alexis Built the World's Best E-Skateboard—Now He's Taking It Global

After perfecting the board and closing out a successful Kickstarter, Alexis is now shifting his focus to scaling production and expanding globally. I recently rode the board with him in Copenhagen—yes, it lives up to the hype—and heard about the rollout he's planning.

Behind the Story

Reed Langridge
Founder, Qava
Alexis Chabat
Founder, Liquid Skateboard

Focus Areas

Product Design 🛠️
Go-To-Market 🚀

We share stories of partners who turn ambition into action—hoping to inspire others or offer the practical know-how to do the same. This month, we spotlight Alexis, the Founder of Liquid Skateboard.

What began as a passion project in his garage has become the most authentic electric skateboard on the market. Alexis stayed loyal to the experience of riding a traditional skateboard—bringing in technology only where it enhanced the natural feeling of cruising. Recently, I had the chance to ride his board with him in Copenhagen, and the experience was unlike anything else.

Here's how Alexis went from prototype to production—and now sets his sights on the world.

  • 1.The Beginning
  • 2.Building the Best
  • 3.Scaling Production
  • 4.Going Global
  • 5.The Future

The Beginning

After years of riding regular boards and testing nearly every electric skateboard available, Alexis kept running into the same issues: great speed but poor handling, solid range but weak build quality, smart features but inconsistent performance. Nothing felt complete.

I just wanted something that felt real under my feet.

Driven by that simple idea, Alexis began building his own board. After countless prototypes, long nights in the garage, and endless trial-and-error, he landed on a design that felt real—smooth acceleration, responsive braking, and highly stable.

From that point on, friends asked to buy one. Strangers stopped him at skateparks. Word spread. Liquid Skateboard was born.

Early design iterations

Building the Best

Perfecting the world's best electric skateboard took a lot of iteration. Alexis spent months refining motor configurations, experimenting with batteries, adjusting deck shapes, and testing materials. The shelves in his workspace are lined with the materials, devices and lessons he has accumulated over the years.

The result is a board that combines performance, durability, and an authentic riding feel:

  • ⚡️ Up to 28 mph with smooth acceleration
  • 🔋 About 25 miles of range on a single charge
  • 🪶 Lightweight yet solid construction
  • 🌊 A ride experience that felt intuitive and natural

Early adopters noticed immediately. Reviews began appearing on YouTube and Reddit. Within six months, Alexis had sold more than 200 boards—each one hand-built and personally tested.

But demand was growing faster than the company's operations could manage.

Liquid components & materials

Scaling Production

Moving from a one-at-a-time build to true manufacturing is the hardest part of any hardware journey.

Alexis had mastered the engineering—but scaling operations, setting up supply chains, and maintaining quality-at-volume required new focus and new partners.

He began mapping out how to scale production:

  • 🔍 Identifying reliable component manufacturers
  • ⚙️ Creating repeatable assembly processes
  • ✅ Setting quality-control standards
  • 🎯 Designing a production workflow that preserved the board's integrity

Kickstarter became the turning point. The campaign validated demand and provided the capital needed to move from prototype to production runs.

Today, Alexis is deep in this phase—balancing quality with scale, transitioning from handcrafted batches to manufacturing at true volume.

Going Global

With demand in the US rising, Alexis began hearing from riders across Europe and Asia asking when they could buy the board.

Global expansion brings new complexity—import rules, safety certifications, fulfillment logistics, and localized marketing. Alexis is now preparing to establish global distribution channels, prioritizing early interest from Germany, Japan, and the Nordics.

The mission: Take an authentic, beautifully engineered ride and make it available worldwide.

Preparing for scale

The Future

Liquid Skateboard is still just getting started.

Alexis is now exploring:

  • 🏙️ A lighter commuter model for city riders
  • 🌎 Distribution partners in South America and Southeast Asia
  • 🏢 A flagship experience center where riders can test boards
  • 🎓 Potential campus-mobility partnerships with universities

Through it all, one principle hasn't changed:

Build something you'd want to ride every day.

From garage experimentation to a growing global brand, Alexis' story shows what happens when passion, craftsmanship, and perseverance collide.
