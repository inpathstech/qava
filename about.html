About Us

The future of work isn't coming.
It's already here.

I started Qava in 2024 after seeing the same pattern again and again — founders and operators stuck, and incredibly smart talent stuck without the right opportunities to break in.

So I ran a pilot. No code, no automation — just matchmaking. I had more than 100 conversations with MBAs, grads, and career-changers ready to contribute, and over 100 conversations with startups, nonprofits, and growth companies hungry for help.

The result of these matches?

🤝 Sales closed.
🚀 Capital raised.
💰 Grants awarded.
🔓 Careers unlocked.

That pilot proved that when you connect the right minds to the right problems, everyone wins. So I built Qava to make that connection scalable, affordable, and simple.

What is Qava?

Qava is an AI-powered platform that connects top talent with growth opportunities — fast.

We specialize in clearly scoped, high-leverage work:

  • 📊 Financial modeling
  • 📝 Business plans
  • 🚀 Go-to-market strategy
  • 🔍 Customer segmentation & market research
  • 🧭 Pricing, operations, & competitor analysis
  • 💼 Internships, fractional roles & one-off strategic projects

Whether you're launching something new, scaling what's working, or cleaning up a mess, Qava brings you the thinking power to move forward — without hiring full-time or paying bloated consulting fees.

Why it works

We stripped out everything that slows people down:

  • ❌ No expensive retainers
  • ❌ No "spray-and-pray" job boards
  • ❌ No ghosting or unclear expectations

Instead, we give you:

  • ✅ Talent that's vetted, motivated, and MBA-trained
  • ✅ Projects with real outcomes, not just tasks
  • ✅ Public proof of work to help talent get noticed
  • ✅ Tools to help companies find the right fit, faster
