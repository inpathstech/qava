Our Story

It was never a talent problem.

We started Qava because we believe in the limitless potential of connecting the right minds with the right tools on the right opportunities.

Again and again, we watched founders and operators hit the same wall: too many priorities, too little trusted support, and no clear path from strategy to execution.

At the same time, we met exceptional people on the sidelines — MBAs, graduates, career-changers, and parents returning to work — ready to contribute, but locked out of the rooms where their thinking could actually matter.

It was never a talent problem. It was a connectivity problem.

So in 2025, we ran a pilot. No code. No automation. Just smart people matched with smart people, project by project, with outcomes tracked honestly.

What we learned was simple: when the scope is clear and the incentives are aligned, work gets done faster, better, and with more trust on both sides.

That pilot became Qava.

What We Are Building

Three parts. One idea.

Great work needs three things: an opportunity worth your time, the tools to move fast, and people who will tell you the truth about your thinking. Qava is building all three.

01

Projects

Opportunities worth your time. A job board is only as good as what is on it. We are deliberate about what gets listed: work that is genuinely impactful, clearly scoped, and often at mission-driven companies or with leaders who would not otherwise reach every corner of the professional strategy market. Fewer listings. Better ones.

02

Templates

Skip the busywork. Keep the thinking. Board decks for companies turning over tens of billions. Startup ideation workshops. Go-to-market strategies. Competitor analyses. Financial models. We took the work we have done ourselves, and the craft we learned from mentors, colleagues, and friends, and turned it into templates you can find, download, customise, and make your own.

Storytelling still matters. Communicating a vision still matters. But the slide work should not be the hard part. Spend your hours on the problem, not the formatting — and move forward without blind spots.

03

Community

A place to think out loud. Post an idea. Ask a hard question. Get a real answer from someone who has done it before. No algorithm burying your post, no performance, no infinite scroll. Built to be useful, not addictive — somewhere you leave sharper than you arrived.

Our Values

These are the standards we use to build Qava and guide every project on the platform.

1. Stay Curious

For others, curiosity can feel like a liability. At Qava, we believe an open mind is fundamental to doing great strategic work. We ask better questions, pressure-test assumptions, and stay open to better answers.

2. Do the Hard Work

Great outcomes take hard work, and we embrace it. We value preparation, follow-through, empathy, courage, ingenuity, and drive from everyone who contributes on the platform.

3. Pursue Excellence

We take real pride in being best-in-class. From first brief to final delivery, we focus relentlessly on quality that earns trust and helps people make better decisions.

4. Think Independently

We are not built on groupthink. Independent thinking is core to how we work and how we are building this business. We challenge assumptions and back ideas based on what actually works.
