Partner Network

Share your story, grow your reach, inspire builders.

You probably landed on this page because we reached out and loved what you're building — or you've heard about us and are interested in collaborating.

At Qava, we interview founders, creators, operators, innovators, investors, and corporate leaders building remarkable things or have an interesting approach to business that we think is awesome. Via blog post or video interview, we spotlight your journey, your insights, and your opportunities, and share it with our global community of talent and builders.

Quick intro

If it's a fit, our team will reach out within a few days to schedule a short pre-meet. During that, we'll get to know each other quickly and come up with an agenda for our deep dive. We keep it simple, thoughtful, and founder-friendly — because we know you're busy.

We spotlight people building interesting, ambitious, or meaningful things — regardless of company size or follower count. We care about originality, insight, and lessons others can learn from.

After your approval, our feature partners are highlighted across our platform and shared with our global network of talent, builders, and operators actively looking to learn, collaborate, and contribute.

The job market is in a challenging place, and there's real fear that AI is changing everything. That's exactly why people need to see examples of others chasing their dreams anyway — building, creating, and carving their own path. Your story could be the one that inspires someone to take that leap.
