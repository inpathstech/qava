When Parenting Becomes a Systems Problem—and Finally Gets a Real Answer

When parents ask, "How am I going to get through this?", Lumi provides real-time, science-backed collaboration support designed for the way neurodiverse families actually live.

Behind the Story

Michael
Founder, Lumi
Reed Langridge
Founder, Qava

Focus Areas

Parenting Technology 👪
Neurodiversity Support 🧠
AI-Powered Coaching 🤖
Family Collaboration 🏠

When parents ask that question, they're rarely asking a philosophical one.

They're asking at 7:45 a.m., when the morning routine has already unraveled.
They're asking after homework dissolves into tears.
They're asking when strategies that worked yesterday suddenly don't work today.

For millions of families raising neurodiverse children, these moments aren't occasional—they're constant.

And until now, there hasn't been a reliable in-the-moment answer.

A Problem Families Are Carrying Alone

Some days feel like the same story on repeat:

"Sam, I've asked you three times to put on your shoes — we're going to be late again."

It's not just a routine glitch — it's a communication breakdown between minds that process the world very differently.

On top of this emotional friction, families invest heavily in support — therapy, coaching, tutoring, medical care — often spending thousands every year. Yet advice remains fragmented, episodic, and siloed from the rhythms of daily life.

Parents are expected to translate clinical guidance into real moments. Children are expected to adapt to systems that weren't built for how their brains work. And when things fall apart — as they inevitably do — there's no tool to help families regain footing in the moment.

This isn't lack of effort.
This is a lack of coordination.

The Insight: This Is a Collaboration Problem

Most support tools treat neurodiversity as a single-player challenge — one caregiver, one therapist, one strategy at a time.

But families don't work that way.

Lumi was built to shape the conversation instead of just offering tips. It acts as a real-time coach that translates between how parents think and how their kids learn, helping turn daily battles into breakthroughs with tailored support tools.

Daily life isn't linear, and neither is behaviour. Routines, transitions, attention, and emotional regulation are all intertwined — and to make progress, families need a system that works for everyone's wiring.

Lumi tackles the root cause: communication and collaboration across neurological differences.

How Lumi Works in Practice

At its core, Lumi creates a shared visual system where the whole family sees the same picture of what needs to happen.

Instead of overwhelming week-long calendars or endless reminders, Lumi breaks down the day into manageable, interactive steps children can navigate independently — transforming routines into journeys of agency and accomplishment.

When families understand each other's wiring, conflict becomes connection. What once felt like defiance often reveals itself as differences in processing, attention, and memory — not behavior problems.

Lumi bridges that gap, turning "Why won't you just…" into "What would help here…" — and helping families work with wiring differences rather than fighting them.

Instead of nagging, childcare becomes partnership. Lumi holds routines, reminders, and responsibilities in a neutral space, freeing parents to guide rather than enforce. Homework support adapts to each child's strengths, not one-size-fits-all checklists.

And when families accumulate small wins — wins that are visible and celebrated — momentum compounds. The child who once struggled to remember tasks begins to complete them independently. The family moves from surviving to thriving.

Real Family Transformation

Lumi's website shares stories that make this shift tangible:

"My wife and I didn't have to remind our son to do a single thing — which has probably never happened before. No rushing or yelling to get him ready. He did it all by himself."

Another family said it best:

"This morning was freaking crazy. It was amazing. And I want this every single day."

These aren't marketing platitudes — they're breakthroughs in daily life, where progress is most real.

Why This Moment Matters

Three forces are converging.

First, neurodiverse populations are growing faster than supportive services can scale — and many regions have no local behavioral specialists at all, leaving families waiting months for help.

Second, parents are embracing tool-assisted support that's in sync with life's tempo — not delayed appointments or abstract frameworks.

Third, generative intelligence has matured to the point where expertise can be delivered instantly, adaptively, and at scale — not eventually, but exactly when it's needed.

Lumi meets families where parenting actually happens: in the moment, not on a calendar.

A Systemic Opportunity

Lumi isn't a behavior tracker, a chore app, or a visual schedule tool.

It's a relationship system that integrates understanding, adaptation, and coaching into the flow of family life.

This is not auxiliary support — this is infrastructure for collaboration that evolves as a family does.

A Reliable Answer

Lumi doesn't promise perfect days. It doesn't erase challenges.

What it offers is partnership — real-time, adaptive, and grounded in how families actually interact and communicate.

When parents ask, "How am I going to get through this?", Lumi delivers something far more powerful than tips and reminders:

A system that finally speaks everyone's language.
