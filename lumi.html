The AI Startup Bringing Real-Time Support for Neurodiverse Families

When families ask, "How are we going to get through this?", Lumi offers real-time, science-backed support designed to help neurodiverse households manage daily routines, communication, and challenges more effectively.

Behind the Story

Michael
Founder, Lumi
Reed Langridge
Founder, Qava

Focus Areas

Parenting Technology 👪
Neurodiversity Support 🧠
AI-Powered Coaching 🤖
Family Collaboration 🏠

For neurodiverse families, daily routines often break down.

Mornings, homework, and bedtime can become repeated sources of stress, even when everyone is giving their all. For families raising neurodiverse kids, this is part of everyday life—and most support systems don't help in the moment.

The Problem Families Face

Many families invest heavily in support. In the U.S., millions of parents spend 12+ hours each week managing conflict around everyday tasks like routines, transitions, and schoolwork. On top of that, families often spend thousands of dollars per year on therapy, coaching, tutoring, and medical services.

Yet despite the time, effort, and financial investment, daily life can still feel difficult and unpredictable.

The challenge isn't a lack of resources—it's how those resources show up. Support is often delivered after problems occur, shared in ways that are hard to apply during stressful moments, or limited to short sessions that don't extend into everyday life.

As a result, parents are left to interpret expert guidance on their own, while children are expected to follow systems that don't align with how their brains process information. When routines break down, families have little support in the moment—and end up starting over again.

This isn't about motivation or effort.

It's about having the right support, available when it's actually needed.

Lumi's Point of View

Most tools approach neurodiversity as an individual issue—focusing on the child, the parent, or a professional in isolation. While these supports can be helpful, they often overlook how family life actually works from day to day.

In reality, routines, communication, expectations, and emotional regulation are shared across the household. A breakdown in one area—such as a missed transition or misunderstood expectation—can quickly affect everyone. These moments aren't the result of a lack of effort, but of systems that aren't designed for different ways of processing information.

Lumi is built around this understanding. Instead of supporting one person at a time, it's designed to help the entire family align around shared routines, clearer communication, and practical tools that make collaboration easier during everyday moments—not just during scheduled appointments or check-ins.

What Lumi Does

Lumi is a real-time coaching and collaboration platform designed to support neurodiverse families in everyday life.

It helps families better understand how each person processes information, attention, and expectations, so routines can be built in ways that actually work. Instead of relying on generic plans, Lumi supports families in creating routines that match a child's executive function and adapt as needs change.

Lumi provides guidance when things go off track—not weeks later, but in the moment. Families can see what's working, track progress over time, and reinforce strategies that lead to smoother days.

Lumi begins by learning how a family works through short assessments and ongoing use. Based on that understanding, it suggests routines, strategies, and adjustments tailored to each household.

When a routine breaks down, Lumi helps families reset before frustration escalates. Over time, the system continues to improve by learning which approaches are most effective across similar situations, helping families move forward with greater confidence and consistency.

How This Changes Daily Life

Through Lumi's easy-to-follow dashboard and platform capabilities, families can manage routines, respond to challenges as they happen, and see meaningful improvements in how daily life feels and functions.

When families understand each other's wiring, friction transforms into empathy

Routines become clearer and more predictable. Children know what's expected, and parents spend less time managing every step. With real-time prompts and guidance tailored to each child's needs, repeated arguments decrease and emotional fatigue is reduced for everyone.

When the system holds the reminders, parents return to being guides rather than guards

Many parents see their children become more independent. Because routines are designed around how a child processes information, kids are better able to follow through on their own. Small wins—getting ready on time, finishing homework—happen more consistently.

Communication also improves. When Lumi holds routines and expectations in a neutral system, parents don't have to act as constant enforcers. Conversations shift away from nagging and toward guidance and problem-solving.

When small wins accumulate systematically, the family momentum shifts

Over time, families report having more energy for the parts of life that matter most. Less effort goes into managing chaos, and more goes into connection, rest, and enjoyment.

The goal isn't perfect days. It's making everyday life more manageable.

Why Lumi Matters Now

Demand for neurodiverse support is growing. ADHD, autism, anxiety, and learning differences are being diagnosed more frequently, but access to specialists has not kept pace.

As a result, many families face long waitlists, limited local resources, and high ongoing costs. Even when support is available, it often comes in short sessions that don't extend into daily life.

At the same time, parents are already turning to technology for help in real time. They want guidance that fits into everyday routines and responds when challenges arise—not generic advice delivered days or weeks later.

Lumi is built for this reality.

Where Lumi Fits

Lumi sits within a large and growing market for pediatric behavioral support and family-centered tools. In the broader context of children's mental and behavioral health, global pediatric mental health markets are projected to expand significantly over the next decade — for example, estimates suggest the pediatric mental health market could grow from around $450 billion in 2026 to over $900 billion by 2035, at a roughly 5.5% CAGR as awareness and demand increase.

More specifically, the $95 billion U.S. pediatric behavioral therapy market reflects the current scale of services like therapy, coaching, and clinical support for conditions such as ADHD, autism, anxiety, and related differences. Lumi does not seek to replace therapists or clinicians in this space; rather, it complements existing care by helping families apply strategies in real time, in between appointments, and in the everyday moments where most progress is lived. This kind of real-time, accessible support is what families need most when routines break down, and clinicians are not physically present.

By providing structure, coordination, and moment-by-moment guidance, Lumi helps families make therapeutic progress part of their day-to-day life — an approach increasingly necessary as the underlying demand for pediatric behavioral support continues to grow.

The Team Behind Lumi

Lumi is built by a team with deep experience in software, AI, design, and education—and firsthand understanding of neurodiverse family life.

They've led 0-to-1 products and built complex platforms at companies like Juno and WeWork. Now, they're applying that experience to create practical tools that help families navigate daily routines with less friction and more confidence.

A Clear Goal

Lumi doesn't promise to eliminate challenges.

It helps families:

  • • Reduce daily conflict
  • • Improve communication
  • • Build routines that actually work
  • • Support independence and growth

When families wonder how they're going to get through the day, Lumi offers a practical answer:

Support that shows up when it's needed.
